Ten-mouse global analysis reveals a shared outcome→late response structure, with flexible predictive loading.

This page consolidates the strongest currently supported neural results across ten real animals: five current VTA mice, three named 2021 mice, and two legacy 2017 mice. Data quality, statistical power, and experimental design are treated separately. The main cross-animal VTA result is a reproducible outcome-to-late response structure; the stronger claim of a kinetics-independent sustained latent state is explicitly not made.

Named 2021 cohort

Cross-animal state geometry is context-specific and strongest in hunger.

Original MATLAB selection authority confirms the identical vtSel vector across hunger, social and control within each animal. The geometry survives representation changes and leave-one-trial deletion.

Hunger is the reproducible cross-animal context.

Historical-normalized state-RDM mean ρ=0.721, exact global state-label null p=0.0020. Raw-baseline-z remains positive with mean ρ=0.560, p=0.0063.

Not every context is shared.

Social geometry is not reproducibly shared across the three animals. Control is intermediate and representation dependent. Cell bootstrap puts hunger above social in 99.96% of resamples in both representations.

Animals: ANM492241 (38 same-selected cells), ANM496190 (44), ANM496191 (23). Across all nine animal×context matrices: exact expected frame count, 100% finite values, zero flat cells.

Legacy 2017 cohort

State geometry survives removal of generic cell responsiveness.

The legacy 319-cell matrix resolves to two real animals. The two ANM372321 lineages are within-animal robustness units and are never counted as two animals.

Cell-centered geometry

After subtracting each cell's mean response across all 11 states, ROC cross-animal RDM ρ remains 0.824 / 0.851; N01 remains 0.747 / 0.738.

Cell-z geometry

After cell-wise z-scoring across states, ROC remains 0.789 / 0.808; N01 remains 0.739 / 0.715. State-label permutation remains significant and cell-bootstrap intervals remain positive.

Single-state robustness. Removing each state in turn, including Fear, does not abolish the cross-animal or within-ANM372321 geometry.
Interpretive boundary

The five-mouse result is a response-structure claim, not proof of an autonomous sustained latent state.

This distinction is kept in the main text rather than hidden in a supplement.

Raw delayed coupling remains positive in 5/5 animals at 8–10 s, but AR(1)-style removal of slow fluorescence carryover weakens the residual relation. PDF

Calcium-kinetics caveat

In the high-trial MAIN sessions, AR(1) positive-innovation coupling is no longer significant for assumed calcium-decay constants τ≥1 s. Therefore the data support a reproducible outcome-to-late response organization, not independent evidence for a kinetics-free sustained neural state.

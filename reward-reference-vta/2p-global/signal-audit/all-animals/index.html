Biological sensitivity: D6 event effect

High method agreement does not mean subtraction is biologically neutral. In ANM54 D6, raw-to-CaRMA event-effect correlation is 0.941, raw-to-Suite2p is 0.974, while CaRMA-v3 versus Suite2p is 0.987. Of 16 cells, raw-to-CaRMA changes the sign of 6, raw-to-Suite2p changes 4, and 3 of the 4 raw-significant cells lose significance after either correction. Thus the main uncertainty is shared biological signal in the local background, not which of the two accepted correction geometries is used.

In eight condition-verified sessions (164 cell×session entries), the raw-versus-corrected response direction can change, but most flips occur for near-zero effects.

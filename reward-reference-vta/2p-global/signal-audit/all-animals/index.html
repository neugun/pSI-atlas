Exact CaRMA v3 vs Suite2p across all source-auditable sessions

1149/1149 trial files passed across 20 sessions, 5 mice and 830 cell×session rows. The fixed cellular ROI is held constant; only the local-background definition and subtraction rule change.

Critical method-definition correction. Exact CaRMA imaging uses ExtractVOlResp_SubRing_v3: a 20-pixel disk around the ROI centroid, exclusion of all fixed ROIs after a 2-pixel dilation, and coefficient 1.0. The earlier immediate 2-pixel ring belongs to ExtractVOlResp_Exclude_SubRing; it is not the v3 authority and is now retained only as a rejected control.
Historical reconstruction is exact. Wherever frozen checkpoint traces exist, CaRMA v3 reconstructs them at r=1.000. Suite2p is also extremely close: median checkpoint r=0.993, range 0.985–0.9998. The rejected 2-pixel ring is much worse: median r=0.788, range 0.680–0.954.

Main result

Across all sessions, CaRMA v3- and Suite2p-corrected traces are nearly identical: session median r=0.994, range 0.992–0.998. Median residual absolute coupling to each method's own background is 0.0385 for CaRMA and 0.0376 for Suite2p. Corrected traces retain 0.973× and 0.977× the raw temporal SD, respectively.

What this solves: the earlier large CaRMA-vs-Suite2p discrepancy was caused by using the wrong CaRMA geometry, not by a true disagreement between the accepted methods. What it does not solve: two agreeing background methods can still both remove spatially shared biological activity. Therefore raw ROI fluorescence remains visible and biological conclusions must be tested under raw, exact CaRMA v3 and Suite2p in parallel.

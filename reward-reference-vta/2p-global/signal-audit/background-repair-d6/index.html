When background contains cell-like structures: raw → location → subtraction → activity

Actual registered movies, 60 trials, 16 existing ROI identities across planes 3 and 4. Each cell has its ROI geometry, local-background pixel footprints and 100E / 100E+Q trial-averaged fluorescence before and after two subtraction strategies. These are one-mouse sensitivity tests, not a declaration that the new correction is validated.

Source fidelity: original raw and historical 20-pixel-disk background were independently extracted from registered TIFF frames and match the stored extraction (r ~ 1, numerical roundoff only). A signal-source correction cannot be trusted unless this reconstruction holds. Unresolved: structural candidates are generated by image local-contrast peak detection and watershed; Cellpose CPSAM-v2 pretrained weights were subsequently recovered from the local cache and loaded. This specific result is the earlier independent watershed structural control, not yet Cellpose-derived correction. This page does not label structural candidates as Cellpose-validated cells.

Where did each method take background pixels?

Historical CaRMA v3: first fill and close each historical ROI, exclude overlapping ROI pixels, then take a disk of radius 20 pixels around its original centroid, excluding every reconstructed ROI plus a 2-pixel buffer. Average these pixels over time and calculate F_raw − 1.0 × F_local. The 20-pixel disk is not a hollow annulus; it becomes irregular after excluding ROI masks.

Suite2p-style comparison: grow outside the ROI with inner neuropil exclusion approximately 2 pixels, extending until at least 350 usable neuropil pixels; exclude other marked ROI pixels. Stored comparison uses F_raw − 0.7 × F_neuropil. This is the independently implemented Suite2p-style mask, not a claim of an original Suite2p stat.npy segmentation for each historical cell.

Structural-exclusion test: use the same original 20-pixel disk, but additionally exclude image-derived candidate bright cellular structures found independently from the registered mean image using Gaussian local contrast, local peaks and watershed, dilated by a safety margin. Re-extract F_clean_bg from the original movies and display F_raw − 1.0 × F_clean_bg. This tests spatial contamination, without selecting the subtraction coefficient from condition-effect preservation.

Distant-background control: use a local ring of radii 20–40 pixels, excluding original ROI masks and structural candidates. It changes distance as well as mask cleanliness; therefore its agreement with raw is not sufficient proof of correctness.

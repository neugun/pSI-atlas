## Manuscript consequence
Behavioral equifinality should not be written as “the models cannot be distinguished.” A better statement is:
the models can be distinguished under generative recovery, but several remain similarly plausible from real behavior; neural timing and causal perturbation therefore identify which latent computation is biologically expressed.

## Boundary
This is strong model/algorithm recovery, not complete numerical parameter recovery. Do not claim individual learning rates, gamma, or belief-alpha are identifiable until dedicated parameter recovery is complete.

# SOE global goal, evidence chain and next decisive experiments (2026-10-07)

## What this paper must explain
**How does a mouse recognize when another mouse provides useful information, selectively sample it, transform the *content* of that observation into its own feeding, and update the strategy on the basis of experienced outcomes?** The answer must bridge behavior, a comprehensible multiscale learning state, neural dynamics, causal manipulation and a generating agent. The aim is not to win a model-zoo table, maximize an RPE P value or build a generic behavior decoder.

The biological principle is **information acquisition ≠ information use ≠ reward/credit learning ≠ motor feeding**. These operations are coupled, but can be perturbed and computed separately.

## A causal-testable circuit of ideas
1. **Acquisition of a socially useful phenotype.** Learner SRI, action conversion and the genuine non-learner group. Primary question: did training improve *use* of what was seen, beyond the amount of viewing? Keep SRI/shuffle-based learner labels and the learner/non-learner trajectory, active/passive/unrewarded events, familiarity and visual controls.
2. **When is observation informative?** Observer state (including near spout), demonstrator feeding and history gate observation policy. Do not assume looking at the demonstrator is always useful.
3. **What was seen becomes feeding.** Separate the Observe information-sampling controller from a native Feed controller dominated by self-state. The bridge uses the recent *demonstrator-feeding content*, not mere Observe recency. The currently source-audited held-animal evidence is a +7.54 pp Feed-hazard difference within 3 s, +6.09 pp after self/current-social residualization; this is an association, not proof of cue causality. An earlier two-policy agent extension is a partially autonomous bridge, NOT a fully autonomous native Feed demonstration.
4. **How results reorganize future sampling.** Current Active/Passive/Unrewarded outcome changes the next choice on a short timescale, history of ~20–33 events spans minutes, and real chronological day order provides a temporary cross-day prior. SLM is an interpretable *representation and update hypothesis*, NOT proof that direct policy outperforms Feature-Q/actor-critic.
5. **What does VTA DA implement?** First compare matched outcome-conditioned DA on its biological time axis. Preserve Early policy and Middle APE and frozen Post 0–6 s RPE benchmarks. Treat the *historical* 1,371-event DA_ActionBout_AUCperSec as a long post-anchor **model interval** (median 28.85 s) rather than a true observation bout (median 2.40 s). The old 9/9, P=.0039 credit-vs-two-weights result is exploratory; on the same 821 genuine-observation events, the model interval beats the two fixed weights 8/9 (P=.0078) and 8/9 (P=.0117) but improves beyond a no-credit history baseline in just 6/9 (P=.5703). True observation DA mostly PRECEDES the Post outcome anchor and must be tested for sampling, not post-outcome teaching.
6. **Does a circuit perturbation change the correct step?** Visual Block tests access to social *content* while observation continues. VTA/Jaws tests post-success Active-belief updating and Observe→Active conversion; current accumulated credit updates decline, but this alone cannot isolate one unique learning equation. OXT/chemo target upstream mechanism only after day/laser condition authority and learner status are verified. NAc/DMS DA release is a distinct downstream readout, NOT interchangeable with VTA neural activity.
7. **Can a model generate the phenotype and predict perturbations?** A compact Artificial-SLM agent with Observe policy and separate self-state Feed policy plus short sensory-content eligibility should generate native Observe→Feed events and then update future sampling. Require independent held-animal trajectories, SRI-like behavior, social-content manipulation, real VB and JAWS matching (not manual feeding labels). SWM provides independent prospective-efficacy/relational-state tests and negative controls; it does not replace the user's own SOE. External tasks/species test **transfer of component computations** (source-tagged credit, state-gated information value, multiscale state, sampling-vs-action separation), not a claim of a single validated global circuit.

## Audit triggered by the 28.85-s mislabeling
- The historical 9/9 result is a comparison between model values in an **extended event interval**, not DA during genuine observation.
- Previous «DA predicts next Observe» claims require a **strict temporal-exclusion test**: 315 of 1706 fixed 0–6 s event rows are reached by the next event earlier than 6 s; 738 of 1363 *valid extended DA rows* end AFTER the next anchor. Contaminated extended averages cannot be used to predict the already-occurring next action.
- Matched 821-event source correction and full stats: [VTA source correction](VTA_TRUE_BOUT_SOURCE_CORRECTION_v126_20261007.md).
- Old results must stay visible as a historical exploratory side test (not deleted), with their exact model comparator and proper error-status boundaries.

## The next decisive analysis queue (do not drift into minor plotting)
**P0: data design:** One authoritative animal × cohort × anatomical site × learner status × day × Normal/ON/OFF/condition × source-event table. Check original FP clocks, laser logs, and source imaging. A failed condition gate blocks biological interpretation.

**P1: direct Observe→Feed mechanism:** Align actual observed demonstrator feeding and its end to the observer's next *true Feed onset*, hazard by 0–3 / 3–10 / 10–30 s, track same animals and prior states, compare self-only/current-social vs observation-recency vs demonstrator content vs structured SLM. Test learner versus nonlearner, Visual Block and contingent JAWS as **separate observational and perturbation questions**. Use true onsets and held-animal CV.

**P2: independent outcome-stage DA:** Frozen event IDs; fixed 0–2 / 2–6 s and actual offset windows; no contamination from next Observe/Feed; rate-of-change and AUC variants; include pre-outcome motion/location, duration, outcome identity, session time and history. **Increment over no-credit/outcome-history baseline first**; selected-vs-fixed credit is only a secondary sensitivity. Split genuine observation signal (usually pre-anchor) from Post outcome signal.

**P3: three-link intervention:** Gross observation intact? Active conversion changed? Model-defined state update changed? Report Normal→Full vs intra-stim OFF→ON and contingent×noncontingent interaction with animal-level paired statistics. Does JAWS actually affect the Feed bridge or primarily future sampling? Make that an empirical test.

**P4: Artificial-SLM mouse:** Native two-action closed loop, feeding feedback to state, virtual causal lesions and out-of-sample validation. The agent should generate *what the mouse does* and the perturbation response, not merely decode one latent.

**P5: presentation:** Each main claim carries the true independent unit, held-animal comparator, result panel(s), negative/boundary interpretation and its place in the full story. English/Chinese stay in sync; plot conventions are visual QA, not a scientific endpoint.

## Authoring decision
Top-level manuscript statement: **“Social learning is a learned ability to sample and use social content for self-action; outcome-dependent teaching restructures future sampling.”** No claim yet that a specific scalar DA credit variable alone explains the Observe→Feed link. The revised VTA source audit improves scientific credibility by separating pre-observation, Post outcome, and long modeled-event averages.

## 2026-10-07 strict neural-to-next-choice gate (v131)

This gate tests the article's *learning affects future behavior* link; it is not the primary demonstration of Observe→Feed conversion. In the raw FP event table, next event timing overlaps **315/1706 (18.5%)** valid fixed 0–6 s DA windows and **738/1363 (54.1%)** historical extended-interval windows. Thus neither naive DA readout is inherently a pre-next-choice predictor. Source [event-overlap CSV](../data/SOE_VTA_next_choice_event_overlap_v131.csv).

With the identical nested leave-animal-out behavior baseline and only an additional current DA residual: (a) fixed post-anchor 0–6 s with next-event gap ≥6 s gives **1391 events, 5/9 improving, two-sided P=.5703125**; (b) extended interval not crossing the next event (but possibly touching its onset boundary) gives **625 events, 1/9 improving, P=.12890625**. The latter is explicitly boundary-touch exploratory, NOT a strict 0.1-s separated prospective test; only two events pass that stronger interval gap gate. Source [recomputed model contrasts](../data/SOE_VTA_next_choice_timing_QC_v131.csv). Neither result establishes independent neural teaching-to-future-choice prediction, but neither disproves circuit-level causal teaching measured separately using JAWS. Prioritize actual content→native Feed and correctly timed intervention endpoints rather than rescuing a DA significance claim.

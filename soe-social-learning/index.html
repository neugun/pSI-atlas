From observation to action: social information foraging, dopamine timing and causal credit assignment

Animals learn when social information is worth sampling, carry its consequences across multiple timescales, convert sampled content into their own action, and assign credit to social outcomes. The default SLM turns these operations into testable variables; SWM tests which parts emerge in a data-driven recurrent state, VTA and JAWS test their neural and causal roles, and external datasets test which computations recur under matched evidence tiers.

Additional behavioral analyses

Active orienting carries predictive social information

Demonstrator feeding/spout state is highly predictive, but observer-side head direction, facing, angular velocity, social turn and social gaze nearly match the all-feature classifier. Social gaze is the strongest single observer-side feature in the manuscript lineage.

Learning reweights an existing behavioral manifold

Successful active-reward bouts are preceded by distinct states. The global manifold remains broadly stable, while occupancy shifts toward socially informative motifs with training.

Conversion progress tracks learning

Conversion progress correlates with ΔSRI (ρ≈.426, P=.0086), directly linking successful information-to-action transfer to behavioral learning.

Learning dynamics

The timescales were recovered independently before the final SLM. Each scale answers a different behavioral need: immediate outcome adjustment, integration of recent social history, and continuity across sessions.

Primary predictive model

The default behavioral SLM is the mechanistic SLM state plus one generic choice-persistence term

SLM asks which compact variables are sufficient to explain social-information sampling while remaining testable in neural and causal experiments. Choice persistence absorbs generic serial choice bias; it is part of the default behavioral readout and is not interpreted as the social-learning mechanism itself.

What SLM means · SLM (Social Learning Model) asks how current state, memory, social-information sampling and outcome-specific credit jointly determine future choice. The mechanistic core contains current state, multiscale memory, sampling policy, source/outcome-specific social credit and value. The default behavioral SLM adds one generic persistence branch. This is the model used for behavioral prediction; neural and causal analyses read out the mechanistic core.

Data-driven validation and extension

SWM separates common decision coordinates from a distinct prospective social-efficacy state

SLM starts from explicit hypotheses about state, policy and social credit. SWM is a recurrent Social World Model trained without those SLM equations. The critical test therefore has three parts: whether SLM-like coordinates are accessible, whether that subspace has a selective behavioral function, and whether SWM contains additional social information that SLM coordinates do not capture.

QuestionWhat does a flexible world model add beyond SLM?
What we compareUse SLM+current-cue baselines, social replacement/no-social controls, and matched-random latent-subspace scrub.
MeaningSWM contains an SLM-aligned policy subspace plus a distinct prospective-efficacy state.
What would count as independent validation? · Decoding alone is insufficient because raw and PCA-compressed encoder states can also reconstruct SLM variables. Functional perturbation is stronger: remove the SLM-coordinate subspace from held-out SWM latents and compare the behavioral loss with matched random subspaces. A separate prospective-efficacy axis can then be tested against current cues and against social-content perturbations.

Additional future dynamics

Integrated interpretation. The data support two separable pieces. SLM-like coordinates form a policy-relevant task subspace used for Observe choice. SWM additionally learns a prospective relational-efficacy variable, ΔActive = P(Active|z, Observe) − P(Active|z, Other), which predicts whether sampling social information will improve the learner's own action and adds Active-vs-Unrewarded ranking information beyond SLM prestate plus all current social cues. The efficacy variable depends on correct social content and is functionally separable from the SLM-coordinate policy subspace.

Generative sufficiency / AI

Generative tests localize where SLM adds structure beyond simpler agents

Held-animal prediction shows that SLM variables forecast choices. A closed-loop agent asks whether the same policy and update rules are sufficient to regenerate the organization of social-information sampling when generated outcomes recursively update the internal state.

QuestionCan the fitted rules regenerate behavior when run forward?
What we compareFreeze baseline, Q, belief and SLM controllers in the same social-foraging world and compare generated trajectories and local strategies.
MeaningSLM's clearest generative advantage is local social strategy and state organization, not blanket superiority on every global metric.
Why an agent is needed · One-step prediction can succeed without reproducing the dynamics that generated learning. The generative test freezes each candidate controller, places it in the same empirical social-foraging environment, and compares the trajectories and local strategies that emerge.

Flexible recurrent policy

Biological interpretation. The generative evidence is strongest for the organization of local social-learning strategy and behavioral-state occupancy. Global trajectory prediction is already captured well by simpler Q/belief agents. A separate recurrent learner provides convergence evidence that the same SLM coordinates compactly describe a policy found by flexible optimization.
Why VTA comes next · Generative sufficiency establishes that the variables can organize behavior. It does not establish where or when the biological system expresses them. The next section therefore asks whether policy, APE and post-outcome credit appear in VTA in the temporal order predicted by one Observe episode.

Neural adjudication

A single RPE account explains part of outcome DA; the full episode requires sampling policy, APE and post-outcome credit

The critical evidence is temporal. Early and Middle windows occur before the social outcome is available, whereas reward prediction error and social-credit updating become defined after outcome.

QuestionAre the computations expressed in the correct biological time order?
What we compareTest pre-outcome policy, middle APE, and post-outcome update against RPE/choice-kernel/black-box alternatives.
MeaningRPE explains part of outcome DA; the full episode requires the ordered policy→APE→post-outcome sequence.
Why three readouts? · The actor first assigns a probability to Observe. APE is the sampling action prediction error, actual action − pre-action policy probability, measured after the sampling decision. Once the observation outcome is known, reward/value and source-specific social credit can update. These readouts follow the chronology of one Observe episode.

Beyond SOE

Cross-task convergence is strongest at the computation level, with different evidence tiers across datasets

External datasets are organized by the specific operation they can test: source-tagged credit, state-gated information value, task-matched multiscale state, and sampling-to-action separation. Population-level comparator tests, component tests, nested sensitivities and architectural analogues are kept separate.

QuestionWhich computations repeat outside SOE?
What we compareUse independent-subject/rat strong comparators where possible, and label macaque/component/analogue evidence separately.
MeaningThe transferable claim is computation-level convergence with explicit evidence tiers.
What counts as conserved computation? · A computation earns stronger support when it clears a matched alternative in an independent held-out animal or subject. Component-level tests show that one operation transfers. Nested primate results establish within-dataset consistency. Architectural analogues identify a similar task decomposition without claiming full replication.

Current cross-task conclusion. The strongest population-level external support is for task-matched multiscale state in Human and Rat. Source-tagged credit and state-gated information value have convergent component evidence across several social tasks. Sampling-to-action separation remains a major direct SOE result with external architectural analogues. Frozen-core macaque transfer shows reusable recurrent dynamics within a two-monkey dataset; it establishes task-dependent representation transfer and leaves population-level primate confirmation to additional animals.
